Privacy Policy for CapGen AI

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CapGen AI handles information when you use the CapGen AI Android application.

2. Information you provide

When you generate a caption, you may enter a topic and select a language and style. The topic, language, and style are sent to the CapGen AI backend so that an AI-generated caption can be created.

3. How we use information

We use the information you submit to provide caption-generation functionality and operate the service.

4. AI processing and service providers

CapGen AI uses third-party infrastructure and AI service providers to process caption-generation requests. Your caption-generation input may be transmitted to our backend hosting provider and to OpenAI for AI processing.

CapGen AI does not use your submitted topics or generated captions for advertising or to build a personal profile.

5. Storage of saved captions

If you use the Save/Favorite feature, saved captions are stored locally on your Android device using the app's local storage. They are not intentionally uploaded to our backend as part of the Save feature.

Caption history shown during a session is maintained locally by the app and is not intended to be stored on our server.

6. Clipboard and sharing

If you choose Copy, the selected caption is copied to your device clipboard. If you choose Share, Android's sharing system lets you select another app or service to receive the caption. CapGen AI does not control how that third-party app handles the shared content.

7. Information we do not intentionally collect

CapGen AI does not require an account and does not intentionally collect your name, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone recordings, or payment information through the app.

8. Advertising

CapGen AI does not currently display third-party advertising and does not use an advertising SDK.

9. Security

Requests between the app and our production backend are transmitted using HTTPS. We take reasonable technical measures to protect information processed through the service. No internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data retention and deletion

CapGen AI does not intentionally maintain a user account or a server-side profile. Locally saved captions remain on your device until you remove them through the app or uninstall the app.

Caption-generation requests are processed to provide the requested result and are not intentionally stored by CapGen AI as a user history database. Third-party service providers may maintain information according to their own policies and applicable security or abuse-prevention requirements.

11. Children's privacy

CapGen AI is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's data practices or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published at the same public URL with a new “Last updated” date.
